项目案例 · 香港大学Case study · HKU

太平洋咖啡收购估值。Valuing Pacific Coffee.

作为财务顾问小组，评估一家中国饮料企业收购国际咖啡连锁的战略价值。报告分六部分，每人负责一部分，我负责第四部分：财务评估，也就是下方的估值与融资方案。Acting as a team of financial consultants, we assessed a Chinese beverage group acquiring a global coffee chain. Each member owned one of six sections; I owned Part IV, the financial evaluation: the valuation and financing plan below.

市场Market

稳步增长的蓝海A steadily growing market

全球咖啡市场年复合增长 4.52%；中国的城镇化、中产扩张与年轻消费者推动需求。Global coffee grows at a 4.52% CAGR. Urbanisation, a growing middle class and young consumers drive demand in China.

战略Rationale

华润 × 太平洋咖啡China Resources × Pacific Coffee

借华润的零售网络、物流与供应链降本增效，补齐集团饮品布局。China Resources’ retail network, logistics and supply chain cut costs and fill a gap in its beverage line-up.

风险Risks

夹在两强之间Squeezed between two giants

瑞幸与星巴克双寡头挤压，品牌有老化风险；对策是差异化定位与双品牌协同。Luckin and Starbucks dominate and the brand risks looking dated. The answer: differentiated positioning and a dual-brand play.

我负责的部分 · 第四部分My section · Part IV

在没有财报的情况下，怎么定价？How do you price a company with no financial statements?

我们用三种方法互相印证：以瑞幸、星巴克单店效率为基准推算经营价值，用 309 家门店的资产做价格底线，再把协同效应按 20% 审慎折算。单位：百万元人民币。We cross-checked three methods: operating value benchmarked on Luckin and Starbucks per-store efficiency, an asset floor from 309 stores, and synergies counted at a prudent 20%. Figures in RMB million.
